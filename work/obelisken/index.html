Obelisken

A tower with a depth camera and a projector. It measures the body in front of it and projects the depth data, and graphics attached to it, back onto that body in realtime: camera and projector calibrated to each other, about 100 ms from photon to photon.

Orbbec Femto Bolt, openFrameworks.
